# Getting Started

PEtFiSh is an AI companion that runs alongside your AI coding assistant — in every interaction, not just when called.

## What PEtFiSh Does

Most AI skill tools are passive: you install them, call them, and hope the agent remembers they exist. PEtFiSh takes a different approach. Its **Companion Gateway** runs automatically before every message, providing:

- **Context guarding** — detects topic drift and prevents cross-topic pollution
- **Gap sensing** — spots missing capabilities before they cause failures
- **Failure recovery** — catches errors from the previous turn and recommends fixes
- **Anti-sycophancy** — pauses before blindly agreeing with your assumptions

On top of this always-on layer, PEtFiSh provides **12 skill packs** covering course development, deployment ops, research, presentations, writing style, and more — with 96 skills total across 8 AI platforms.

## Who It's For

PEtFiSh is for developers and teams who use AI coding assistants (OpenCode, Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Copilot, Windsurf, Antigravity) and want:

- **Structured workflows** — not ad-hoc prompting, but repeatable skill-driven processes
- **Quality guardrails** — lint, security audit, and trust governance for AI skills
- **Cross-platform consistency** — same skills work across 8 platforms
